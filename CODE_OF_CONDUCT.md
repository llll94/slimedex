# Code of Conduct

Slimedex follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project and its community (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions and the Slimedex Discord) a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- being respectful and kind to other players and contributors;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- insults, trolling, harassment, or personal or political attacks;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- submitting false or malicious data to the community database;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and community contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior by opening an issue at https://github.com/llll94/slimedex/issues or by messaging the maintainer on the Slimedex Discord. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
